Method

How the benchmark was built

1. Pick 20 items people actually ask about

Everyday packaging plus three hazard items: vapes, AA batteries and LED bulbs.

2. Hand-check 8 cities

Toronto, New York City, Vancouver, Shibuya, Berlin, Camden, San Francisco and Phoenix. Each rule records the bin, the city's own stream name, a verbatim quote and the URL.

3. Draft 17 more cities with a model, then filter hard

A rule is kept only if its quote appears word for word on the page. A quote reused for four or more items is a category line, not an item rule, so it's dropped.

4. Spot-check

Of 50 random auto-drafted rules, 41 were correct, 4 ambiguous and 5 wrong. The 5 wrong rules were removed.

5. Score

Accuracy, accuracy on hazard items, and the share of drop-off-only items sent to a normal bin. The location-blind ceiling gives every city the most common answer for each item.

Limits

  • 14 of 31 official pages couldn't be fetched without a browser (blocked requests, JavaScript widgets, moved PDFs).
  • Rules change. Quotes reflect pages collected in November 2026.
  • Most official Indonesian guidance is category-level. Makassar publishes item examples, so it is on file; it is not in the scored set.
