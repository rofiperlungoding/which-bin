Frequently asked questions

Why not just ask ChatGPT or Gemini?

They give the same answer everywhere. When cities disagree, as they do on 17 of 20 items here, any location-blind answer tops out at 78%.

Where do the rules come from?

Only official city or council pages. Every row in the dataset links to its source and quotes it word for word.

What if my city isn't covered?

Add a row with a quote and the official URL. bench.py checks it. Missing cities get "check with your city", not a guess.

How accurate is the dataset?

94 rows were checked by hand. 206 more were drafted by a model and kept only when the quote matched the page exactly. A spot check of 50 of them found 41 correct, 4 ambiguous and 5 wrong, and the wrong ones were removed.

What about rules changing?

Pages are saved with the date they were collected. Running fetch.py again refreshes them.
