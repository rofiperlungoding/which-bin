How it works

Four steps, no guessing

  1. FetchSave a copy of each city's official waste pages, so every answer comes from the same text.
  2. ReadThe model answers using only the city's own words, not general knowledge.
  3. QuoteEvery answer comes with the sentence it relied on.
  4. VerifyA quote is shown only if it appears word for word on the page. Otherwise the answer is marked as a guess.

Frequently asked questions

Why not just ask ChatGPT or Gemini?

They give the same answer everywhere. When cities disagree, as they do on 17 of 20 items here, any location-blind answer tops out at 78%.

Where do the rules come from?

Only official city or council pages. Every rule in the dataset links to its source and quotes it word for word.

What if my city isn't covered?

The checker says so instead of guessing. You can add your city with one row in the dataset: a quote and the official URL.

How accurate is the dataset?

94 rules were checked by hand. 206 more were drafted by a model and kept only when the quote matched the page exactly. A spot check of 50 of them found 41 correct, 4 ambiguous and 5 wrong, and the wrong ones were removed.

What about rules changing?

Pages are saved with the date they were collected. Fetching them again refreshes every answer.
