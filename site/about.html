About

Recycling is local.
Advice should be too.

Which Bin started from one question: if everyone now asks AI where things go, whose rules is it answering with?

The problem

The same pizza box, three answers

In New York a greasy pizza box goes in the compost. In Saint Paul it goes in recycling. In Philadelphia it goes in the garbage. Each city is right for its own trucks, plants and contracts.

General advice, from a search snippet or a chatbot, can't be right for all three. For batteries and vapes, a wrong answer can start a fire.

What we believe

1

The city is the authority

We only use official city and council pages. No blogs, no aggregators, no model memory.

2

Show the words, or say you don't know

Every answer quotes the page word for word. If there's no matching sentence, we say it's a guess.

3

Open by default

The dataset, benchmark, server and site are all public, so anyone can check, fix or extend them.
